Privacy Policy for Fake Data Filler

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Overview

Fake Data Filler generates fictional placeholder information and fills matching form fields only when you use the extension. It is designed for testing and demo purposes.

Data collection and use

Fake Data Filler does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store your personal information, browsing history, website content, or form submissions. Generated placeholder data is created locally on your device. The extension reads only the visible form-field metadata needed to identify matching fields and fill them at your request.

Local storage

The extension stores your selected appearance setting (System, Dark, or Day) locally in your browser. This setting is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Third parties

Fake Data Filler does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, remote code, or third-party data services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the extension's data practices change. Any update will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue in the Fake Data Filler GitHub repository.
